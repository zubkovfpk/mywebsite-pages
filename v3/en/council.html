Council 7+1 · AI diagnostics platform

The platform analyses. Experts review. The chief designer signs.

The platform makes analysis fast and thorough. Legal, financial and industry specifics are additionally reviewed by invited experts: a lawyer, a finance specialist/economist and an industry specialist. The bureau's chief designer signs the assessment.

Human signature

The bureau's chief designer reviews the findings and signs the assessment.

Why humans: the platform is evolving, and the market needs time to trust AI-native assessments. That is why in three roles the agents' findings are reviewed by invited experts. As verified assessments accumulate, the agents' share of the work grows, but the signature and accountability remain with the bureau.

How it works

From the first question to the signature

  1. 01

    Clarification

    The lead agent asks questions until context completeness reaches the sufficiency threshold.

  2. 02

    Briefs

    A deterministic orchestrator sends briefs to the seven expert roles. It is code, not another model.

  3. 03

    Cross-check

    Findings of the AI roles are compared; contradictions are not smoothed over but recorded as dissenting views.

  4. 04

    Expert review

    A lawyer, a finance specialist/economist and an industry expert review and complement the findings in their fields.

  5. 05

    Signature

    The bureau's chief designer consolidates the assessment, signs it and is accountable for it.
