FAQ

On money and boundaries

How much does it cost?

Diagnostics starts at RUB 500,000 per fixed-scope phase. The fractional CAIO costs RUB 180k–300k per month. Design supervision for one project costs RUB 80k–160k per month. Implementation is estimated once the truly necessary scope is clear. If parts of your processes do not need AI, you learn it during diagnostics, not at the end of the budget.

What if AI turns out to be unnecessary?

That is not a refusal but a saving: you get a decision matrix and a conventional-automation roadmap – and spend the budget on what works. For the bureau it is as successful a project as an implementation.

Who does the work?

Council 7+1 prepares the analysis. Legal, financial and industry findings are reviewed by invited experts: a lawyer, a finance specialist/economist and an industry specialist. Sergey Zubkov leads diagnostics and architecture and signs the assessment. The bureau's DS/ML engineers implement, with no anonymous subcontractors.

Can we implement in-house or with another contractor?

Yes. We check the work against the assessment and architecture, review deviations and report on whether each phase is ready for acceptance.
