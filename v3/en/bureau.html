Principles

Six rules of the bureau

  • The right to say “no” – a “no AI” verdict is as valid an outcome as an implementation
  • Signature – every assessment has an accountable person
  • No subcontracting – our own team implements, no anonymous contractors
  • Only familiar industries – we do not advise where we have no practice
  • Verifiable numbers – every metric has a method and assumptions
  • Design supervision – we stay accountable for the result even when your contractors implement

Accountability

What we are accountable for

  • Assessment – the soundness of every verdict: technology, economics, law, industry
  • Architecture – the target solution design and the limits of AI autonomy
  • Numbers – the method and assumptions behind every effect estimate
  • Design supervision – conformity of the implementation with the assessment – whether by our team or by external developers and contractors
  • Governance – monitoring, KRIs and a human in the loop on critical decisions
