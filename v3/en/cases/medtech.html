1Task

A digital dental microscope manufacturer needs an AI adoption program that raises device value and sales – without changing the optics.

2Result

A rollout sequence is defined: non-diagnostic features in 6–12 months, the clinical assistant after dataset collection and validation, then AR overlay and telementoring. The client receives a strategy for taking a new niche, including an export track.
