KOR.CASE.08

Drilling and pumping equipment · reliability

+RUB 17.8 mln per semi-sub season · payback from ~13 h

An assessment of drilling rig processes: where AI is justified and where only deterministic automation belongs.

Functions
AI strategy and portfolio · Operations and AI-agent autonomy control
Processes
15 drilling-work scenarios – from maintenance and supply to alerting and reporting
Roadmap
budget spread across seasons and technologies, payback from year one
Boundary
AI outside the critical-infrastructure perimeter · models on board · to shore – events only

Task

A shelf drilling operator needs to determine where AI delivers measurable value on the rigs and where it is barred by industrial safety and critical-infrastructure rules.

Result

The launch order is set: the first maintenance pilot goes to semi-submersibles, where returns are positive already in the first season; jack-ups join at rollout. The verdict matrix contains a risk register under security and compliance vetoes.
