Industries

Six industries where we know the context from the inside

The industry expert is one of the seven diagnostic roles. So we only take industries where the bureau has its own practice.

Industries

Where we work and which verdicts are most common

Mining and hydrocarbons

Oil, gas and ores. Safety and logistics in harsh environments: offshore, ice, remoteness.

Typical: AI in video analytics and failure prediction; no AI in safety permits and procedures.

Urban infrastructure

Inter-agency integration and city data: 60+ agencies, closed environments, public procurement rules.

Typical: unified data and integration first; AI in citizen requests and Earth observation analysis.

Construction and development

The whole project life cycle: surveys, design, construction, operation, sales.

Typical: hybrid in document flow; no AI in cost estimates and reconciliations.

Maritime logistics and insurance

Fleet and risk: weather support, insurance coverage control, economics of rare but large events.

Typical: AI in ice conditions and application triage; no AI in premiums and warnings.

Agriculture

Geo-ML field monitoring: from aerial imagery to results for the inspector in 2 hours instead of 3 days.

Typical: AI in image detection (mAP@0.5 > 80%); defer where there is no yield history.

Environment and ESG

CO₂/CH₄ emissions monitoring, climate risks, nature-based solutions on Earth observation and AIS data.

Typical: hybrid — the model estimates, the methodology and auditor confirm.

Honestly about what we do not do

Where we do not go

FinTech, iGaming and industries where we lack years of practice. Advising where you do not know the ground is dishonest: those industries have their own specialists.
