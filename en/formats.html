FAQ

On money and boundaries

How much does it cost?

Diagnostics is a fixed price per scope. Implementation is estimated once the truly necessary scope is clear. If you do not need AI, you learn it during diagnostics, not at the end of the budget.

What if you decide we do not need AI?

That is a normal and frequent outcome. You get a decision matrix and a roadmap for conventional automation and save budget on what you do not need. For the bureau it is as successful a project as an implementation.

Who does the work?

Council 7+1 prepares the analysis. Legal, financial and industry findings are reviewed by invited experts: a lawyer, a finance specialist/economist and an industry specialist. Sergey Zubkov leads diagnostics and architecture and signs the assessment. The bureau's DS/ML engineers implement, with no anonymous subcontractors.

Can we implement in-house or with another contractor?

Yes. Then the bureau provides design supervision: it checks that the solution matches the assessment and architecture, accepts phases and tracks metrics.
